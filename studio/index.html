Before we begin

A little clarity.

Do the package fees include renovation?

No. The listed prices are design fees for the specified deliverables. Furniture, materials, and construction need a separate scope and quote.

Can I use my own builder?

Yes. You can discuss handing the completed design scope to your own builder, or explore coordinated renovation through SECHA.

How do I confirm a project?

Send your brief to the studio on WhatsApp. Availability, final pricing, and the project agreement are confirmed directly with the team.

Can SECHA help with financing?

Explore the financing route on the main SECHA site. All applications, terms, and approval decisions are handled by Amar Bank.
